Publications

Our research team has published extensively in leading scientific journals. Below is a selection of our recent work.

2025

Bidirectional Neural Interfaces: Progress and Ethical Considerations

Hayes, E., Crawford, J., Patel, A.
Nature Neuroscience, Vol. 28, Issue 3, pp. 245-258

This review examines recent advances in bidirectional brain-computer interfaces, discussing both technological innovations and ethical frameworks necessary for responsible development of neural enhancement technologies.

Neural Plasticity Following Interface Implantation: A Longitudinal Study

Chen, S., Hayes, E., Klein, M.
Journal of Neural Engineering, Vol. 22, Issue 1, pp. 116-132

Analysis of long-term neural adaptation in patients with implanted neural interfaces. Results indicate significant plasticity and functional reorganization over 24-month follow-up period.

2024

Machine Learning Approaches to Neural Signal Decoding

Chen, S., Crawford, J.
IEEE Transactions on Neural Systems, Vol. 32, Issue 6, pp. 892-905

Novel deep learning architectures for real-time decoding of motor intentions from neural signals. Achieved 94% accuracy in cross-validated trials with minimal latency.

Comprehensive Neural Mapping Using Advanced fMRI Techniques

Chen, S., Klein, M., Hayes, E.
Brain Mapping, Vol. 45, Issue 8, pp. 1234-1256

Detailed mapping of neural pathways using high-resolution functional MRI and diffusion tensor imaging. Results provide new insights into brain connectivity patterns.

Ethical Frameworks for Human Enhancement Technologies

Patel, A., Hayes, E.
Bioethics Quarterly, Vol. 38, Issue 2, pp. 145-162

Proposes comprehensive ethical guidelines for research and clinical application of neural enhancement technologies, with emphasis on informed consent and patient autonomy.

2023

Biocompatible Materials for Long-term Neural Implants

Klein, M., Hayes, E.
Biomaterials, Vol. 298, pp. 122-135

Investigation of novel polymer coatings for neural electrodes to reduce immune response and improve long-term stability of implanted devices.

Cognitive Enhancement Through Non-invasive Brain Stimulation

Crawford, J., Patel, A.
Cognitive Neuroscience, Vol. 14, Issue 4, pp. 567-581

Systematic review of transcranial magnetic stimulation effects on memory and executive function in healthy adults. Meta-analysis of 47 studies.
