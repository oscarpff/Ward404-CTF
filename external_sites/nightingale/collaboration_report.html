Executive Summary

This report outlines the ongoing partnership between Nightingale Research Institute (NRI) and Hospital Saint Anima (HSA), located in Madrid, Spain. The collaboration focuses on clinical data collection for Project ANIMA, our flagship neural interface research initiative.

⚠️ Note: This partnership operates under a confidential research agreement. Public disclosure of patient data or research methodologies is prohibited without prior authorization from both institutions.

Project ANIMA - Clinical Phase

Under this partnership, Hospital Saint Anima provides clinical trial facilities and patient recruitment services for Project ANIMA. The collaboration began in July 2025 and has successfully enrolled 15 participants in Phase II trials.

Research Focus: Neural interface tolerance testing, cognitive enhancement protocols, and long-term biocompatibility studies of implanted neural electrodes.

Data Transfer Protocol

All patient data, including fMRI scans, biometric recordings, and neural signal patterns, are securely transmitted from HSA to NRI facilities via encrypted HTTPS connection (port 9443). Transfer logs indicate successful transmission of approximately 2.4 GB of medical imaging data in October 2025.
