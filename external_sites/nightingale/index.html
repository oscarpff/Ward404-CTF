Welcome to Nightingale Research Institute

We are a leading independent research organization dedicated to advancing neuroscience, neural interfaces, and cognitive enhancement technologies. Our multidisciplinary team works at the intersection of neurobiology, computer science, and medical ethics.

Our Mission

At Nightingale Research Institute, we believe in the transformative potential of neuroscience to improve human lives. Our research focuses on understanding the brain's complexities and developing innovative technologies that can enhance cognitive function, treat neurological disorders, and push the boundaries of human-machine interaction.
